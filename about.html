ABOUT

Galman Ferguson is a writer of horror and speculative fiction based in Indonesia.

His work explores isolation, memory, guilt, and the unsettling spaces between what we know and what we cannot explain.

He writes psychological, folk, and cosmic horror, often drawing horror from ordinary places and situations before gradually pushing them into stranger territory.

His stories are less interested in simple monsters than in the things people carry with them — the memories, fears, and choices that refuse to stay buried.

This website is a home for his fiction, publications, and other writing projects.
