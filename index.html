ABOUT THE WEBSITE

"Noli timere mortuos, quia nocere non possunt. Time vivos, quia possunt."

This website was once the ambition of a budding, wide-eyed young writer in the 2000s who was a little naive, totally oblivious to the actual horrors of real life, and sometimes slightly too overconfident in his approach to finding the so-called “horror factors” that could be used and infused into his writing process.

What makes a good horror story? I have often asked myself that. This morbid fascination with the unknown has been crawling around in the dark corners of our minds since the first human walked the earth and began wondering whether there might be more to the world than meets the eye.

Fear has ruled our world since the first spark of consciousness took root in our minds. Fear of death, loneliness, darkness, disease, loss, and even the most insignificant embodiments of it are all there to remind us that it is deeply ingrained in us to seek comfort and safety.

And it was in the comfort and safety of my living room that most of the stories on this website first came to life in their earliest drafts. It is easy to scare yourself for fun when you are not in a position to be scared of anything.

For all the places my writing could go, the process rarely strays far from the realm of psychological realism. I tend to gravitate toward character-driven stories because I find that they create a more immersive and relatable sense of experience. Not to mention that sometimes the scariest and most disturbing things in life happen mostly inside your own head.

I hope these stories give you a really good scare, in the same way they did for me while I was writing them. Perhaps they will even make you look over your shoulder when you are alone in the middle of the night, only to laugh it off and think how lucky you are that you are not in one of them.

Good wishes,

Galman
